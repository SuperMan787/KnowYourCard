About this tool. CardMatch scores cards using publicly known, general-market eligibility bands, fees and reward structures for illustration — issuers revise interest rates, fees, joining offers and income cut-offs often, and exact eligibility also depends on your credit score, city and existing relationship with the bank. Treat this as a shortlist to research further, not a final decision. Always confirm current terms on the issuer's own website before applying. This tool doesn't collect, store or transmit anything you type — all matching runs locally in your browser. Nothing here is financial advice.
